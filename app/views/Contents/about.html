About 6502.org

6502.org has been a gathering place for 6502 enthusiasts since 1999. The site is home to a large archive of technical documents, many links to projects and resources, a source code library, tutorials, and a forum where hobbyists and engineers help each other build projects with the 6502 and its relatives.

Maintainers

The people who keep 6502.org running:

Mike Naberezny Mike Naberezny

Mike created 6502.org and is still working on it. He made its first HTML pages on his C128 and uploaded them over dial-up. Mike still uses the C128 sometimes but has broadband now.

Garth Wilson GARTHWILSON

Garth is the moderator of the 6502.org forum. He serves as the first line of defense against the spambots. When he's not doing that, Garth is lending his technical expertise to others.

Ed Spittles BigEd

Ed has been a prolific contributor all over the site, making it a little better with every link he adds or broken one he fixes. Ed is often the first to welcome new members on the forum.
