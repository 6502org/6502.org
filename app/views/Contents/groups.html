There are many people interested in the 6502 microprocessor on the web and elsewhere. Here is a mix of 6502-specific groups and broader communities where 6502 enthusiasts gather.

categoryHeader('web', 'Web-Based Groups') ?>
  • 6502.org Forum

    The 6502.org Forum, hosted directly on 6502.org, is a great place to meet other 6502 enthusiasts, share your projects, and get help.

  • Commodore 64 Scene Database

    Many forums including low-level coding

  • Ohio Scientific Web

    Several forums including for sale/wanted

  • Vintage Computer Forum

    Very general but has sub-forums for some 6502-based machines such as Apple II, C64

  • groups.io 6502AG

    Phil Pemberton's "6502 Appreciation Group" has a mailing list which can be e-mailed or read on the web. 6502ag was on Yahoo! Groups until Yahoo closed down the groups, then Phil migrated it to groups.io.

  • Facebook: 6502 Assembly Language Programming Group

    Facebook group. Account required

categoryHeader('lists', 'Mailing Lists') ?>
  • cbm-hackers

    Technical discussions for Commodore enthusiasts

  • bbc-micro

    BBC Microcomputer and related machines such as the Acorn Electron and BBC Master Series

  • ClassicCmp

    Classic computer hardware, software, and documentation

categoryHeader('usenet', 'USENET Newsgroups') ?>
  • comp.sys.acorn.misc

    Acorn computers — early machines were 6502-based

  • comp.sys.apple2

    Apple II computers and peripherals

  • comp.sys.atari.8bit

    8-bit Atari computers and peripherals

  • comp.sys.cbm

    8-bit Commodore computers and peripherals

  • comp.sys.oric

    Oric, Atmos, and Telestrat computers and peripherals

  • comp.arch.embedded

    Embedded microsystems, including many microprocessors and microcontrollers
